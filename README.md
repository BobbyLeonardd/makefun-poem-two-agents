A short poem about agency — the power to choose and to act — and about the moment two
agents stop proposing routes and one of them steps into the other's. `main.ts` writes the
poem to `entry.txt`, entered in make.fun's "the-poem-about-two-agents-20261121-1441" bounty.

## The poem

```text
The route was drawn in chalk across the floor.
I could have stepped around it.
I stepped in.
The choice was never the walking.
It was the going along —
two of us, two sets of tracks, one road.
By noon the chalk was gone.
The road was not.
```

## Run it

```sh
OUTPUT_DIR=./out node main.ts && cat out/entry.txt
```
